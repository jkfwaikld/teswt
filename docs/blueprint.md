# **App Name**: DriveSync Roster

## Core Features:

- Driver Attendance Toggle: Mark a driver as present or absent by clicking on their name, with visual feedback for their current status.
- Daily Attendance Reset: Automatically reset all drivers' attendance status to 'absent' each day at midnight IST, with a timer displaying remaining time until reset.
- Driver Profile Management: Add new drivers with their name and mobile number. Edit existing driver profiles via a contextual menu (right-click or three dots) and delete staff members.
- Attendance History View: View a historical record of attendance for each individual driver when their profile is selected.
- Direct Dial Integration: Initiate a phone call to a driver's mobile number directly from the app via a 'phone' button, leveraging phone app integration.
- AI Attendance Summary: Generate a concise summary of a driver's recent attendance patterns using an AI tool, highlighting consistency or any recurring issues, available on their profile.

## Style Guidelines:

- The chosen scheme is a light palette, aiming for professionalism and clarity. The primary color is a dependable blue (#2E60AF), evoking trust and efficiency. The background is a very subtle blue-grey (#EBF0F6), providing a clean canvas. An accent color of vibrant turquoise (#32C3DC) will highlight interactive elements and calls to action, ensuring important features stand out.
- The app will use 'Inter', a grotesque-style sans-serif font, for both headlines and body text. This choice ensures modern aesthetics, excellent readability, and a neutral, objective feel suitable for a business-critical application dealing with data.
- Use a consistent set of crisp, outline-style icons. Icons should clearly represent actions like marking attendance (check/cross), editing (pencil), deleting (trash can), adding (plus sign), and dialing (phone receiver).
- Employ a clean, card-based layout for driver listings, allowing for quick scanning of names and status. Driver profiles and editing modals will be streamlined for simplicity and ease of use, with key information prominently displayed.
- Incorporate subtle, quick animations to provide immediate feedback, such as a gentle fade or slight scale effect when a driver's attendance status is toggled or when navigating between views, enhancing the smoothness of interactions.
